Refunds and donations

Version 1.0 · Last updated 27 October 2026

Short version: the tool is free, nothing is sold on this site, so there is nothing to refund. Donations are voluntary gifts and are not refundable — except when a charge is a mistake, and then we refund it.

1. There is nothing to buy

  • No price, no plan, no subscription, no trial. Every tool — library, simulator, comparison, calibration, validation and every export — is free for anyone with a free account.
  • No hidden fees. No fee to export, no fee to remove a watermark, no fee to lift a limit, no processing fee, no "premium cells", no ads you can pay to remove.
  • No payment details are collected. This site never asks for a card, bank account or wallet. If anything inside the app ever asks you to pay, it is not us — please tell us using the contact address on the legal notice.

2. Donations are voluntary gifts

  • Donating is optional. It pays for hosting, datasheet digitisation, validation measurements, accessibility work and translations.
  • A donation never unlocks a feature, a cell, a model or an export, and it never buys influence over what the library contains. Everyone gets the same tools whether they donate or not.
  • Nothing is given in return: no goods, no service, no access, no receipt of value. That is why a donation is not a purchase.
  • Donations are non-refundable. Once a donation has been paid, it is not returned, except in the cases in section 3.

3. When we do refund

If a donation went wrong, we refund it. Write to the contact address on the legal notice page, ideally within 30 days, and include the date, the amount and the platform you used. We refund in these cases:

  • you were charged twice, or charged an amount you did not choose;
  • a recurring donation was taken after you cancelled it;
  • a charge was not authorised by you;
  • you donated by mistake — for example you meant to donate once and set up a monthly donation. Tell us within 14 days and we will refund it, no argument.

We refund through the same platform that took the payment, so the money goes back the way it came. The platform's own processing time then applies.

4. Cancelling a recurring donation

Recurring donations are managed by the platform that hosts them — for example GitHub Sponsors, Open Collective or Liberapay — and a link to manage or cancel yours is in your account on that platform and in the emails it sends you. Cancelling stops future payments from the next billing date; period already paid is not returned. We cannot cancel it for you, and we never see your payment details.

5. Your statutory rights

A donation is a voluntary gift with nothing supplied in return, so the usual consumer withdrawal right for purchases of goods or services does not apply to it. Nothing on this page removes a right that the mandatory law of your country gives you; where that law says you are entitled to a refund, it wins over this page.

6. If you think a donation was not handled properly

Contact us first — most things are settled in one email. If you and we cannot agree, you can use the complaint route of the donation platform, and any consumer dispute body available to you under the law of your country. Those details are on the legal notice page.
