5. The engineering and safety disclaimer — please read this one

Library parameters are illustrative reference values, not manufacturer data. Each cell entry combines published datasheet quantities with chemistry-level model parameters. Until an entry has been validated against measurements, its parameters are a starting point, not a specification.

Calibrate before you rely on a number. The calibration tool refits the model on your own measured profiles. That is what makes a model trustworthy for your cell, at your currents, temperatures and age.

Confidence scores are heuristics, not statistics. They flag when a result leaves the datasheet envelope; they are not confidence intervals and they do not bound the error. Known limits — no hysteresis, no constant-voltage phase, lumped thermal model, single ageing factor, no thermal runaway — are listed on the Documentation page, section 14 "What to keep in mind".

Do not use the outputs as the sole basis for a safety-critical design. Battery cells can vent, ignite or fail. Verify any model against measurements and physical testing before you use it in a product, a charger, a BMS, a vehicle or anything else where a wrong voltage can hurt someone or damage property. You are responsible for your design and for the decisions you take with these numbers.
